# Brain Cruncher

Brain Cruncher is a B&E Digital stereo resonator and drone processor born from
the loose-sheet-metal behavior of Occlusion Engine. It deliberately turns the
comb filtering, sympathetic panel modes, stereo phase motion, and signal-driven
rattle into a playable effect rather than a boundary-emulation preset.

Its binaural section creates independently timed left and right signals from a
mono source, then applies virtual ear spacing and source panning for deliberate
interaural time and level differences.

The default profile is the original full-wet brain-crunching sound. Factory
profiles explore gentler binaural motion, metallic drones, brittle percussion,
and extreme noise while the output ceiling remains active.
