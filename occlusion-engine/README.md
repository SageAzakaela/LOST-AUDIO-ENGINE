# Obfuscation / Occlusion Engine

Browser audio processor for next-room, behind-wall, and resonant-body transmission.

The wet path models five distinct physical stages:

1. source-side early reflections;
2. mass and material-dependent transmission loss;
3. material modal resonances plus construction-dependent cavity feedback;
4. sub-echo panel vibration smear and signal-excited loose-contact rattle;
5. listener-side early reflections.

Edge leakage is a separate delayed, band-limited route rather than a dry bypass.
Material controls mass and modal color; construction controls whether that material
is solid, stud-backed, hollow, mounted as a panel, or physically loose.
