            THE WORKFLOW
            
    The shortest useful path
    
            
      
              
    1. Load audio.
      WAV and MP3 remain local to this browser.
      2. 
              
    2. Play or loop a useful moment.
      Tune while the sound moves through the rack.
      3. 
              
    3. Add hardware from the left.
      Drag it into the signal path or press its plus switch.
      4. 
              
    4. Play the device surface.
      Use its large macros and Mix first; click the device for deeper controls.
      5. 
              
    5. Finish and export.
      Use Mastering for delivery, Automation for movement, then export the audible chain.
      6. 
            
    
          
    
          
    
            WHAT MATTERS
            
    Rack rules
    
            
    
              
    LEFT → RIGHT
    Rack position is processing order. Reorder devices by their grip.
    
              
    SURFACE
    The controls you are most likely to perform live.
    
              
    INSPECTOR
    Device models, failure mechanics, presets, and precise values.
    
              
    A/B
    Switch between the dry source and the complete processed path.
    
              
    SAFE STOP
    Immediately ends playback if a state becomes unpleasant.
    
              
    MASTERING
    A separate post-rack stage for cleanup, meters, dynamics, and delivery.
    
            
    
            
    Good starting move
    Choose a rack preset, lower each device Mix, then exaggerate one behavior at a time.
    
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Nothing in this guide is required before you can use the instrument.
